# BOSCH QUANTUM – PLACES & ORGANIZATIONS

## Internal Bosch research locations and units

- **Bosch laboratories (quantum sensors & quantum gyroscopes)**  
  - Focus: Quantum sensors based on nitrogen-vacancy-center diamonds for neurological diagnostics; quantum gyroscopes / yaw‑rate sensors based on nuclear magnetic resonance for highly automated driving.  
  - Relevance for Edu: Primary internal destination for roles in quantum sensing R&D, hardware demonstrators, and application-focused research.

- **Bosch quantum technology coordination (central function)**  
  - Focus: Cross‑cutting coordination of Bosch quantum activities (computing, sensing, and quantum-safe IoT) and alignment with European initiatives.  
  - Relevance for Edu: Strategic vantage point to understand open projects, cross‑business collaboration, and potential internal transfers.

## External organizations and ecosystems

- **European Quantum Industry Consortium (QuIC)**  
  - Type: Europe‑wide consortium of industry players in quantum technologies.  
  - Role: Platform for collaboration between companies, research institutes, and policymakers on quantum computing, sensing, and communication; explicitly mentioned by Bosch as a key collaboration forum.  
  - Relevance for Edu: Indirect path to opportunities via Bosch participation (projects, working groups, and joint demonstrators with universities and startups).

- **European research centers and partner countries**  
  - Countries highlighted by Bosch: Austria, the Netherlands, Switzerland, and other EU member‑state research centers with strong quantum programs.  
  - Role: Collaboration partners for Bosch in quantum computing and sensing applications.  
  - Relevance for Edu: Potential secondments, joint PhD/industrial projects, or Bosch‑led collaborations where internal staff participate.

## How to use these locations for Edu’s internal reallocation

- **Entry points inside Bosch**  
  - Connect with quantum sensor demonstrator teams in Bosch laboratories (neurological diagnostics) and quantum gyroscope teams (automated driving) as concrete technical homes.  
  - Engage with the Bosch quantum technology coordination function to understand upcoming internal projects and staffing needs.

- **Bridging roles and profiles**  
  - Look for roles that combine Edu’s current expertise (e.g., electronics, signal processing, embedded systems, materials, software, or systems engineering) with quantum‑adjacent topics such as sensor readout, control electronics, data analysis, or application integration.  
  - Prioritize projects explicitly referencing quantum sensors, quantum gyroscopes, or quantum-safe IoT in their descriptions.

- **Ecosystem-facing opportunities**  
  - Track Bosch’s activities and working groups within the European Quantum Industry Consortium to identify externally funded projects that require Bosch internal staff.  
  - Explore collaborations with European research centers (Austria, Netherlands, Switzerland, broader EU) where Bosch is already active in quantum technology.
