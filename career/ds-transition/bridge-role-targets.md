## Application filter (score before apply)

For each opening, set `senior_ds_on_team` = 1 only if JD or recruiter confirms a senior DS / ML lead reviews models. Else 0.

**Apply if:** title is PL DS / Applied DS / ML Analyst / DS I–II **or** Senior DA with modeling + mentor **AND** geo Tier A/B **AND** pay band can hit R$10k+.

**Skip:** pure Senior DS; modeling-heavy DE; solo “cientista de dados” who is lonely BI; foreign DA unless remote BR pay converts cleanly.

## Success criteria

- Offer signed **or** internal mobility with DS mentoring.
- One modeling artifact reviewed by a senior (even offline).

## Tracker habit

In job opening `notes`, prepend: `senior_ds=0|1` and salary ask outcome when known.
