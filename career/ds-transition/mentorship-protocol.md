# Mentorship and collaboration framework

## Before applying — senior DS filter

Ask early (recruiter or interview 1):

> Quem revisa modelos no time? Tem cientista de dados sênior ou a vaga é BI com outro nome?

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Named senior DS / ML lead | Prefer; set `senior_ds=1` |
| Only BI / analytics, no model review | Deprioritize for transition |
| Solo DS title, no peers | Usually skip |

Prefer research institutes and AI cells (SiDi, Eldorado, CPqD) and product companies with DS chapters. Use Bosch network for shadowing: propose relevance-score → supervised baseline pairing.

## First 90 days on the job

1. **Weekly 30-min pairing** on your current model — bring errors, not slides.
2. Ask seniors to review **metrics and leakage**, not only style.
3. Volunteer for **monitoring board** of an existing model — start with latency, error, timeout, volume.
4. Keep a learning log: decision | why senior disagreed | what you changed.

## External acceleration (if offer is slow)

One Campinas peer who already ships models **or** one LinkedIn mentor (SiDi/Eldorado alumni) with a fixed monthly ask. Not five parallel courses.
