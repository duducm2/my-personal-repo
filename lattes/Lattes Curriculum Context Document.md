Platform Overview
The Lattes Curriculum is a standardized academic and scientific resume platform maintained by the Brazilian National Council for Scientific and Technological Development (CNPq). It serves as the official national database recording the trajectory of researchers, students, and academic professionals in Brazil.

Core Purpose
The platform is mandatory for anyone pursuing academic progression in Brazil. It is universally required for admission into graduate programs, applying for research grants, and academic employment. Unlike a traditional corporate resume, it strictly emphasizes scientific production, technical research, and academic background.

Essential Sections

Personal Data and Summary: Includes contact information and an introductory paragraph summarizing academic history, current roles, and research interests.

Education: Complete history of degrees, including dissertation titles and academic advisors.

Professional Experience: Academic and corporate roles relevant to technical and scientific research.

Areas of Expertise: Specific scientific domains and language proficiencies covering reading, writing, speaking, and understanding.

Research Projects: Current and past academic investigations.

Productions: Peer reviewed manuscripts, technical productions, software, algorithms, and conference abstracts.

Strategic Application
When building this curriculum, the focus must highlight the intersection of corporate innovation and academic rigor. The profile should prominently feature Master's research on perceived trust in e-commerce, detailing specific methodologies such as web scraping, machine learning, and UI flexibility analysis. Professional experience as a UX Researcher at Bosch should be formatted to emphasize technical contributions and applied scientific methods. Future academic objectives, including doctoral studies in machine learning or quantum computing, must shape the introductory summary to position the profile accurately for advanced research opportunities and international collaborations.
