Openings CRUD

Job openings board only.

ATS means Applicant Tracking System — the software employers use to collect and screen applications (e.g. Workday, Greenhouse, Gupy, LinkedIn Easy Apply). An ATS application is the online form on that system (name, email, phone, education, résumé upload, hiring message, and similar fields). With a job card focused or its dossier open, use Alt+F to type the application tab title and send that window, with your profile and career path, to the AI companion. The import box opens when the send finishes, and importing starts filling that same Chrome window. Alt+C does the same for this opening’s cover letter: send the prompt, then paste the JSON into the import box that opens and build a one-page PDF. Subscribed cards show the date you subscribed, taken from the import. New openings record that date. After 30 days the card turns dark. The ring on the card means you are still monitoring it and restarts that 30-day count. Older openings stay as they are until you ring them.

Pages

  • Job openings — home · pipeline bento board
  • Job portals — market boards only (LinkedIn, Gupy, Greenhouse…)
  • Target companies — employer dossiers. Highlight a card and press Ctrl+Enter to open its careers portal. Pinned companies stay at the top, oldest last opening first, so the ones you have not checked in the longest time come first. The card shows that date when an opening exists. Other companies follow AI recommendation rank within International (I-n) then Regional (L-n) tracks. International means any country outside Brazil (US, EU, Asia, etc.), not US-only; Regional is Brazil / local. Career path filters include UX Researcher, Data Analyst, Product Owner, and Digital Solution Architect. Use the Rec track filter to focus one group; reorder with ↑↓ on the card or Alt+↑ / Alt+↓ on a focused / open company.
  • Career profile — fields & resumes
  • Career advice — which role fits, and the Brazilian salary ask
  • People (Shift+O) — two categories. Both can be tied to a company. Hiring is someone hiring at a company. Network is someone in your own network. Alt+R sends a pasted LinkedIn profile to the AI companion after you pick the category. The import box opens after the send.
  • Hiring 2026 — visual briefing of which job-search channels still convert

Country flags are inferred from region / scope / location text. Foreign HQ with local footprint shows a second BR flag plus a BR industry cue. Combine Country (Brazil or Near home) with Home office → Accepts remote / HO for Brazil employers that post remote or hybrid roles. The same Home office chip also includes international employers that hire Brazilians remotely (GitLab, Automattic, Zapier, Globant, and others — classified Oct 2026). Use Country → International HQ plus Accepts remote / HO when you want non-BR HQ only.
