Openings CRUD

Job openings board only.

ATS means Applicant Tracking System — the software employers use to collect and screen applications (e.g. Workday, Greenhouse, Gupy, LinkedIn Easy Apply). An ATS application is the online form on that system (name, email, phone, education, résumé upload, hiring message, and similar fields). With a job opening open, use Alt+F / Alt+A to generate answers from that form’s page text and import them into the opening’s copy panel for pasting back into the ATS. Use Alt+C / Alt+L in the same dossier’s Cover letter section to generate a cover letter prompt and import the AI JSON into a one-page PDF.

Salary ask (Brazil)

September 2026 pretensão for Brazilian forms. Monthly CLT gross base, before INSS, income tax, PLR, 13th salary, vacation, and benefits. Public aggregates, not an offer.

Number to type: R$ 12.000. Floor R$ 10.500. Stretch R$ 14.000 for São Paulo capital, a product company, or a fintech. R$ 12.000 is the senior data-analyst median plus a modest premium for the master’s, fluent English, and about seven years at a multinational. It stays above a data-scientist pleno median and below a senior-scientist or lead-architect number.

Path Ask Floor Stretch
Senior Data Analyst R$ 12.000 R$ 10.500 R$ 14.000
Data Scientist pleno R$ 11.000 R$ 10.000 R$ 13.000
Product Owner R$ 11.500 R$ 9.500 R$ 13.500
Digital Solution Architect pleno R$ 13.000 R$ 11.500 R$ 15.000

If the form allows only one number, use R$ 12.000. Do not quote the R$ 16.000 senior data-scientist median. Product Owner stays in the pleno-to-low-senior band. Architect pleno stays under the about R$ 14.000 CAGED median unless the posting is explicitly senior. Campinas metro and industrial multinationals: use the default and treat PLR and benefits as part of the package.

PJ only if they ask for a contractor rate: about 30% above the CLT ask (no 13th, vacation, or FGTS). Default R$ 15.500. Data Scientist pleno R$ 14.500 · Product Owner R$ 15.000 · Architect pleno R$ 17.000.

Pages

  • Job openings — home · pipeline bento board
  • Job portals — market boards only (LinkedIn, Gupy, Greenhouse…)
  • Target companies — employer dossiers + careers / ATS links. Default order is AI recommendation rank within International (I-n) then Regional (L-n) tracks (pinned still float to the top). International means any country outside Brazil (US, EU, Asia, etc.), not US-only; Regional is Brazil / local. Career path filters include Data Analyst, Product Owner, Digital Solution Architect, and Data Scientist. Use the Rec track filter to focus one group; reorder with ↑↓ on the card or Alt+↑ / Alt+↓ on a focused / open company.
  • Career profile — fields & resumes

Country flags are inferred from region / scope / location text. Foreign HQ with local footprint shows a second BR flag plus a BR industry cue.
