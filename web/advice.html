Career advice

Which role fits

The role you fit best is Senior Data Analyst, in the applied sense: someone who turns messy operational and experience signals into numbers a leader can act on. That is already the job, under the title Applied Research and Design Analyst.

The proof is repeated. Power BI for projects, resources, training, and a UX-maturity audit. ServiceNow scoring with property weights. Call-center records categorized in Python and shown to stakeholders in Power BI across finance, logistics, and transport. CSI 4.86 and NPS 9.94 on your own projects. The master’s is the same craft: 127 people versus AI-agent scores, inferential statistics, Python into Power BI. Excel and VBA go back to the internship. A hiring manager for a senior analyst can recognize that without a translation layer.

Product Owner is the second door, and only for a certain kind of opening. You already run the path from requirements to prototypes, customer check-ins, governance, and training. That is discovery and delivery. A classic product-owner seat, the one that owns the backlog and the tradeoffs every week, is a thinner match. The general notes say you supported delivery and project management. The product-owner version of the same work says you owned it. Use that path when the posting is about stakeholders, outcomes, and research. Skip it when the posting is a backlog administrator.

Data Scientist pleno is a direction, not the best fit today. You have statistics, Python, and a careful evaluation study. You do not have a record of shipping models: features, training, error analysis, and a model someone else depends on in production. Daily agentic AI in VS Code is how you work. It is not a data-science portfolio. Pleno scientist screens will compare you with people who have that record, and you will lose those screens more often than analyst screens.

Digital Solution Architect is the weakest of the four. The same dashboards, ServiceNow scoring, and Power BI audits are being described as solution design. An architect interview asks who owned the system boundaries, the cloud shape, the integrations, and the non-functional requirements. Earlier web work (React, SQL Server, IIS) is application delivery. Azure is on the skill list. The experience does not show you designing architectures. An internal Bosch move that already knows your domain is more plausible than an external architect search.

If you bet the search on one title, bet it on Senior Data Analyst (business or experience analytics is the same fit). Keep Product Owner for openings that are really about discovery and outcomes. Treat Data Scientist pleno as the next step after you can show one applied model end to end. Put architect applications behind those.

Salary ask (Brazil)

September 2026 pretensão for Brazilian forms. Monthly CLT gross base, before INSS, income tax, PLR, 13th salary, vacation, and benefits. Public aggregates, not an offer.

Number to type: R$ 12.000. Floor R$ 10.500. Stretch R$ 14.000 for São Paulo capital, a product company, or a fintech. R$ 12.000 is the senior data-analyst median plus a modest premium for the master’s, fluent English, and about seven years at a multinational. It stays above a data-scientist pleno median and below a senior-scientist or lead-architect number.
